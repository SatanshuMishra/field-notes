# Changelog

Each release has a section `## [X.Y.Z] - YYYY-MM-DD` with an optional intro paragraph and `### New`, `### Improved` and `### Fixed` lists.

Write the entries in plain English for the people who use Field Notes: say clearly what they will notice, and leave out technical detail such as frame rates, graphics memory figures or the names of the app's internal parts.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.9.0] - 2026-11-11

This release lets you choose when Field Notes reminds you, keeps your notes syncing on their own, and makes the app calmer while it sits open.

### New

- Choose when Field Notes reminds you: in the evening, at midday and in the evening, or at a time you pick on a day timeline. Choose in Settings or while you set up the app.
- If today still needs a bloom or a log, a last reminder at 10:30 PM says what is missing. A reminder time you pick at 9:30 PM or later takes its place.
- Your streak counts a day once it has a bloom and at least one log. Until 4 AM you can still finish the day before, and the streak shows a moon while you can.
- Opening a note or a voice note shows a faint night sky, a little different each time, with the odd shooting star. The voice recorder now follows your light or dark theme.
- Field Notes syncs when it opens and when you come back to it after a minute or more, and a box in the sidebar shows whether your notes are synced. Click it to open Sync and storage.

### Improved

- Pages fade into each other instead of switching abruptly. With reduced motion on, they fade quickly without moving.
- Videos in an earlier day's window have the same player controls as the log viewer, and they fit at every window size.
- An earlier day's window reads as one surface: its two sides line up, one line runs along the bottom of both, and its corners stay round. A past day's bloom names only its flower.
- Sync and storage shows while it syncs, when it last synced and when it can't reach your server, and Sync now waits while you are offline.
- Outside Google Play, Field Notes checks for updates every time it opens and once a day after that, so a new version shows up sooner. Nothing installs until you choose.

### Fixed

- With a bloom picked, the drifting petals kept the whole app redrawing even while you weren't using it. They now float across in occasional gusts with quiet pauses in between, so Field Notes rests while it sits open. Petals during setup fall as before.
- Rounded outlines on cards such as On this day and the update notes no longer lose their corners.
- Hover highlights in Settings, the steps of a day's logs and Cancel now leave room around their words.

## [1.8.0] - 2026-11-10

This release gives notes one calm place to be written, read and edited, and makes earlier days quicker to reach.

### New

- On Today, tap an earlier day in this week's garden to open that day straight away, without going through the Calendar.

### Improved

- Notes open full window in the same writing space you write them in. Reading a note shows its day and time at the top with Edit and More, and Edit, or the E key on Mac and Windows, turns the same page into the editor without anything moving. On Android, Close, Edit and More sit along the top, and Rename and Delete are in More.
- A new note starts with the cursor in its title. Press Return or Tab to move on to the note itself.
- The note editor and the voice and video recorders show the day your log goes into, "today" or "adding to" and the date, in place of the privacy line.
- The voice recorder's button is a flat coral circle in a cream ring, matching the video recorder.
- The window for an earlier day is shorter. Its mood sits on one line, as it does on Today, and on Mac and Windows each log is a compact two-line row. Clicking a row shows it beside the list, where a new full-screen button opens it full window.
- The button that opens a log full window shows the same full-screen icon everywhere.

### Fixed

- If Field Notes stayed open past midnight, Today kept showing the previous day's logs until you restarted the app, and a log or mood added after midnight could be saved to the previous day. Today now moves to the new day at midnight and checks again whenever you come back to the app.

## [1.7.0] - 2026-11-09

This release lets you give any log a title and brings voice notes and videos onto one set of player controls.

### New

- You can give any log a title. Type one while you record a voice note or video, in the message that appears once it is saved, or at the top of a note. Rename a log later from its card's More menu or straight from the viewer. Titles sync to your other devices and are included when you export your journal.
- Voice notes and videos share one player, on the cards and in the viewer, with a timeline, skip back and forward, mute, volume and speed. The volume and speed you choose apply to every recording.
- Hover over or drag along a video's timeline to see a small picture of that moment above it.
- On a touch screen, double-tap the left or right side of a video to skip back or forward 10 seconds.
- On Mac and Windows, every player answers the same keys: Space or K to play and pause, J and L to skip 10 seconds, the left and right arrows to move 5 seconds, the up and down arrows to change the volume, and M to mute.
- In the viewer on Mac and Windows, press F, or double-click a video, to hide everything else and fill the window with it. Escape brings the rest back, and pressing it again closes the viewer.

### Improved

- Notes open in their own writing space with a title at the top. A note that already starts with a heading shows it as its title, and the heading moves into the title the next time you open and save the note.
- Log cards show a title only when the log has one, and every card has Open and More buttons, with Rename, Delete and, for notes, Edit in More. Clicking a video's picture plays or pauses it, dragging its timeline moves through it, and clicking anywhere else on the card opens it.
- The viewer uses the same player as the cards and shows a log's title only when it has one.
- On Mac and Windows, New log opens a taped page with three paper scraps: Voice, Video and Note. On Android, the New log sheet keeps its familiar rows, now with soft shadows under Voice and Video and handwritten captions.
- The voice recorder uses the same record button as the video recorder and, like it, fades its controls away while you record.
- You can make the Field Notes window narrower on Mac and Windows. When space runs short, the sidebar folds up and Today hides its side column; both come back when you widen the window.
- Confirmation questions put their buttons in the order your system uses, and pressing Escape always picks the safe choice. On Android they open as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.
- Search and Settings headings, and the messages they show while loading, when empty or when something goes wrong, now look the same as the rest of the app.
- On Mac and Windows, buttons that show only an icon name themselves in a tooltip, and buttons everywhere are large enough to hit easily.

### Fixed

- On Mac and Windows, clicking with the mouse no longer leaves an outline around what you clicked. The outline still appears when you move around with Tab or the arrow keys.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-11-09

### Improved

- The video recorder moves smoothly between getting ready, the countdown and recording, and the record button looks different at each step.
- A few seconds into a recording, everything fades away except the microphone control and a small timer, so you can focus on what you are saying. Tap the screen or press a key to bring the controls back.
- On Android, recording starts as soon as the countdown ends, without stopping at "Getting the camera ready".
- On Android, a soft, blurred copy of the picture fills the space around your video instead of black bars, and the whole video stays in view.
- The words on the recorder have a soft shadow, so they are easier to read over the picture.

### Fixed

- On Android, the first time you open the video recorder it asks for the camera and then the microphone, one at a time, instead of sometimes staying blank.
- On Android, if you refuse camera access, the recorder shows a screen where you can try again.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-10-27

### Improved

- While a video is still downloading, its caption now sits on a dark backing so you can read it over any picture, and Play shows the download's progress in its place until the video is ready. Screen readers announce the progress as it goes.
- Grey and coloured text is easier to read in both the light and dark themes, and checkboxes, switches and field outlines stand out more clearly.
- Delete buttons look the same everywhere: red text with no fill. In a delete confirmation, pressing Enter chooses Cancel, so you can't delete something by accident.
- On phones, the note, voice and video viewers keep Delete in the top corner, away from Close and the main button at the bottom.
- To-do checkboxes are easier to tap on Android, and you can tick them from the keyboard on Mac and Windows.
- With large text turned on, words wrap onto the next line instead of being cut short or overlapping, in Settings, the calendar, the mood picker and the welcome tour.
- The text over the Meadow, including its header, "Growing your meadow" and the labels in the phone's bottom bar, sits on a soft backing so you can read it at any time of day.
- While a voice note saves, Keep this shows a spinner and can't be pressed twice. If saving fails, a message next to the recorder says what happened and what to do next.

### Fixed

- Day numbers for days still to come on the calendar are now shown in full colour; only their flowers are faded.
- In the welcome tour's month, flowers no longer cover the day numbers when you use large text.

## [1.4.1] - 2026-10-23

### Improved

- The mist over your heaviest stretch in the Meadow now lies low around the flowers' stems, so you can see the flowers of those days at noon, at dawn and at night. A milder stretch gets lighter mist.

### Fixed

- The Meadow no longer shows mist or a "Mist in the hollow" card for a stretch that wasn't heavy, or a "Butterflies" card for a stretch that wasn't bright.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-10-19

### New

- While you record a video, a soft ring around the microphone button grows as Field Notes hears you, so you can see your sound is being picked up. It stays inside the button and never covers the picture.
- If Field Notes can't hear your microphone when you press record, the recording doesn't start and a message asks you to check your input, so you don't end up with a silent recording.

### Improved

- The camera picker in the video recorder opens the same rounded menu as the microphone picker, wide enough to show each camera's full name.
- Buttons in the recorders, pop-ups and Settings now share the same rounded highlight and pointer when you hover over them, and screen readers name each one.
- Menus in Settings widen to show every choice in full and stay inside the window.

### Fixed

- On macOS, sound and picture in video recordings now stay in step for the whole recording.
- On macOS, a video recording no longer loses its sound when the microphone takes a moment to start, which could leave the first recording after opening Field Notes silent.
- While you record, the Input row keeps showing your microphone instead of going blank.
- If the microphone stops partway through a recording, Field Notes now says so and reopens it.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-10-18

### Fixed

- On macOS, Field Notes no longer asks to use your keychain every time you open it. After an update it asks once; choose Always Allow and it will not ask again until the next update.
- The first time you open this version on macOS, you may be asked a few times while Field Notes gathers its saved sync keys into one place. Choose Always Allow each time.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-18

### New

- Choose which speakers or headphones Field Notes plays through, and set its output volume, in Settings > Voice. On macOS and Windows you can also change the output from the recorder's Input panel.
- In the past-day pop-up on macOS and Windows, voice and video logs now open in the right-hand column. Tap a card to open it paused, or its play button to start playing, then go full screen when you want more room.
- Arrows in the past-day pop-up, and in the day sheet on Android, move to the day before or after without closing it.

### Improved

- Background noise is now removed while you record, so a Voice Isolation recording appears as soon as you keep it, with nothing left to process.
- Voice Isolation now keeps a little of the room under your voice, softens hard "s" sounds, brings quiet and loud recordings to an even level, and keeps loud moments from distorting.
- The input level meter moves with your voice in Settings and in the recorder, including while you record.
- Microphone and input profile choices open as a menu under their row instead of a list that pushes the page down.
- On Android, recordings use a microphone source that skips the phone's own noise reduction and volume adjustment.

### Fixed

- Voice Isolation recordings show their waveform again instead of a flat line, including ones saved before this update.
- The prompt in the past-day pop-up's empty right-hand column is now centred.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-15

### New

- On macOS and Windows, you can pause a video recording and carry on where you left off. Keep saves it as one video.
- Choose which microphone Field Notes records from with the new Input button in the voice and video recorders, which also sets the input volume and shows a live level meter. On Android, video recordings use the microphone Android chooses.
- A new Voice page in Settings: Voice Isolation clears background noise like fans, traffic and typing from your voice when you keep a recording of 30 minutes or less, and Studio, the default, keeps your microphone as it is.
- On macOS and Windows, the Input panel shows where sound is playing and opens your computer's sound settings.

### Improved

- On macOS and Windows, the recorder's buttons now sit below the window's title bar, clear of the window's own buttons.

### Fixed

- Turning the camera off before a video recording now turns the camera off and records only your voice. Before, the camera kept recording while hidden.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-14

### Improved

- A short countdown before recording starts.
- A new live visualiser while you record.
- Voice waveforms now show the shape of your recording.

### Fixed

- On Windows, Field Notes no longer closes when you switch back to it while reminders are on.
- Settings now use the same background colours as the rest of the app.
- A voice note in a past day keeps playing when you move the pointer away from it.
- The Restarting Field Notes screen no longer shows a yellow underline under its text.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-13

### Fixed

- On Windows, Field Notes no longer closes unexpectedly while the Meadow is showing, in onboarding or on the Meadow page.
- On Windows, the Meadow page opens much faster, and changing the time of day or switching to full screen no longer makes it stutter or freeze.
- On Windows, the Meadow animation in onboarding plays smoothly instead of stuttering.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-12

### Improved

- Field Notes from Google Play leaves out sync, so it needs no permission to upload in the background. Sync stays in the version from GitHub.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-12

### New

- An About page in Settings shows the version of Field Notes you have, who made it and its licence.
- Check for updates from Settings > About, or let Field Notes check once a day by itself. Nothing installs until you choose.
- On macOS and Windows, an update downloads, installs and restarts Field Notes for you, with your journal and any open draft saved first.
- On Android, Field Notes downloads the new APK, checks it and hands it to Android to install. Copies installed from Google Play open Google Play to update.

### Improved

- Settings on macOS and Windows has a new look: a list of sections on the left, with the open section beside it in clear groups and smaller controls.

### Fixed

- The glow around the voice recorder's orb follows your voice as you speak, instead of breathing on a fixed cycle.
- Recording a voice or video note keeps your phone, Mac or Windows PC awake, so the screen timeout or sleep no longer cuts a recording short.
