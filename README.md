# Field Notes

A journal of days for macOS, Windows and Android.

Field Notes is a private journal. Each day you can write notes, record your voice or a short video, add photos, and choose how you felt. Every feeling is a flower, so each day you choose one grows a bloom, and over the year your blooms fill a meadow.

Your journal stays on your device unless you turn on sync. There are no accounts to make, and no ads, analytics or tracking. The [privacy policy](docs/privacy.md) has the details.

Every screenshot on this page shows Field Notes holding made-up sample notes, recordings and photos.

## Get Field Notes

Download the newest version from the [Releases page](https://github.com/SatanshuMishra/field-notes/releases/latest).

- **macOS**: open `field-notes-<version>-macos.dmg` and drag Field Notes into Applications. The app is not notarised, so macOS stops it the first time you open it. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway beside the Field Notes message, and confirm. After that it opens normally.
- **Windows**: run `field-notes-<version>-windows-x64-setup.exe`. The installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen warns you before it starts: choose More info, then Run anyway. Field Notes installs for your account only and needs no administrator rights.
- **Android**: download `field-notes-<version>-android.apk` on your phone and open it. When Android asks, allow installs from the browser or files app you opened it with, then choose Install.

Each release also has a `SHA256SUMS.txt` file, so you can check a download before you install it. Settings > About checks for new versions, or can check once a day by itself. Nothing installs until you choose.

## Today

Today opens on the day in front of you. Choose how you feel and the day gets its bloom, and change it whenever your mood does. Below it, every log you add today appears as a card in the order you added it: notes, voice recordings, videos and photos. Open a card to read, play or edit it.

On a Mac or PC, the column on the right holds this week's garden, the buttons for a new log, and "on this day", a page from the same date in an earlier year. Click an earlier day in this week's garden to open it. The counter in the sidebar, or at the top on a phone, shows how many days in a row you have kept your journal.

## Voice and video

Record a voice note or a video when words are not enough. A short countdown starts the recording, and you can give it a title as you go or later. On a Mac or PC you can pause a video and carry on where you left off. In Settings > Voice, Voice Isolation removes background noise such as fans, traffic and typing.

Voice notes and videos share one player, with a timeline, skip back and forward, volume and speed. On a Mac or PC, Space plays and pauses, and F fills the window with a video.

## The Meadow

The Meadow is your year as a landscape. Every bloom you have chosen this year grows there as a flower, in a field by a lake under the mountains. Its sky follows the real time of day where you are, from sunrise to sunset to a night of stars and fireflies. Drag to look around, tap a flower to see its day, and use the controls at the bottom to visit another year or another time of day.

## Reminders and settings

A daily reminder nudges you to reflect, and skips itself once the day has its bloom and a log. Choose evenings, midday and evening, or a time of your own, and a last call before the day closes if it still needs one.

Settings also hold the theme and text size, the first day of the week, spell check, your microphone and speakers, sync, and exporting your whole journal as a zip file.

## Sync between your devices

Sync is optional. Turn it on in Settings > Sync & storage to keep your journal on your other devices. The first device needs a sync server address and an invite code, and each device after it joins by scanning a code or typing the eight words another device shows. Everything is encrypted on your device before it leaves, so the sync server cannot read your journal. If you lose every device, your twelve-word recovery phrase brings the journal back.

The copy of Field Notes from Google Play leaves out sync.

## Build from source

Field Notes is a [Flutter](https://flutter.dev) app.

1. Install Flutter 3.47.5 or newer, along with the tools for the platform you are building for: Xcode on a Mac, Visual Studio with the Desktop development with C++ workload on Windows, or Android Studio with the Android SDK for Android. The noise filter for voice recordings is built from C source, so a C compiler is needed too; Xcode and Visual Studio include one.
2. Fetch the dependencies:

   ```sh
   flutter pub get
   ```

3. Generate the code the app depends on:

   ```sh
   dart run build_runner build --delete-conflicting-outputs
   ```

4. Run the tests:

   ```sh
   flutter test
   ```

5. Run the app, or build it:

   ```sh
   flutter run -d macos
   flutter build macos
   flutter build windows
   flutter build apk
   ```

Build for macOS on a Mac and for Windows on a Windows PC. An Android release build made without your own signing key is signed with the debug key.
