- R7 Month picker regression from #209: while the calendar's month picker is open, the Android accessibility tree is empty. Not confirmed with TalkBack. Evidence: a11y-verify dumps/a5-calendar-picker.xml and probe/a5-picker-without-blocksemantics.xml. Bears on c2.
