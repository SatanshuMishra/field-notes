# Open the microphone with the recorder, like a normal camera

Status: accepted

Each take used to open the microphone when record was pressed, and reopening it seconds after the level meter closed it is what made a Bluetooth headset switch modes under the take and stop its sound. The video recorder now opens the microphone with its camera preview and the voice recorder when it opens, keeps it open while the recorder is open, and a take begins writing on that already-open microphone, which also lets the recorder refuse to start while it hears nothing. The cost is that the system's microphone indicator shows whenever a recorder is open, not only while recording.
