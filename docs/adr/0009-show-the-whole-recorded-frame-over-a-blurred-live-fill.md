# Show the whole recorded frame over a blurred live fill

Status: accepted

The video recorder must fill the window with no black sections, yet a person reflecting has to see exactly what is being kept. Field Notes draws the whole recorded frame, contained and centred, over a dim, heavily blurred live copy of the same picture. Cropping to cover was rejected because it hides up to 18% of what is recorded on a tall phone and adds zoom, and fixed panels around a smaller picture shrink the person. The cost is drawing the live camera twice with a large blur, which falls back to the warm stage colour under reduced motion.
