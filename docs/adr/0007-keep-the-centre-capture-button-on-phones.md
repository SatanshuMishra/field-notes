# Keep the centre capture button on phones

Status: accepted

Android's guidance puts a screen's single primary action in a floating action button at the bottom right, and the phone bottom bar's centre + capture button departs from it. The owner kept the centre button because capturing a moment is the heart of Field Notes and the centred button is part of its design; the accessibility sweep records it as an accepted deviation with that reason instead of moving it. Moving it later means reworking the bottom bar and every phone screen's reach zone.
