# Process sound live while recording

Status: accepted

Field Notes cleans, levels and encodes a take's sound while it records, for voice and video, so Keep only closes the file and the log appears at once with its waveform. A pass after Keep made the owner wait in proportion to the take's length, and saved its waveform late or empty; running that pass in the background after saving would have changed the log under the owner. Video therefore records picture only, and Keep joins the cleaned sound to the picture without re-encoding either.
